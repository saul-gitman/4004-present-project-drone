Fly the course, then return home

Collect rings 1 to 5 in order, then land on H. The fence logic turns your stick into a safe velocity using only SPP outputs: p̂, v̂ and σh.

Fly with WASD or arrow keys, or press and hold on the map. L logic on/off, P pause, R restart.

Things to try: push into a fence and let go; fly into the deep canyon (north-east); turn the logic off.

    How the drone is returned

    CLEAR

    vallow ≥ your closing speed. Stick passes through.

    SLOW

    0 ≤ vallow < closing speed. Closing part cut to vallow, sideways kept.

    RETURN

    Verified fix, vallow < 0. Pushed out along n̂ at |vallow| ≤ Vreturn.

    RETRACE

    Fix not verified, vallow < 0. n̂ not trusted: fly back to the last verified fix by integrating v̂.

    HOLD

    No fix, nothing to retrace, or retrace done. Hover until a verified fix.
